BitMakeLab is a system of electronic and mechanical parts, combined with quality learning materials.  Aimed at all age groups, from kids aged 10+ to adults, it makes standard off-the-shelf electronic components much more easily integrated into your digital making projects.

This section includes details of how to built the kits.  All designs are open source.

At the moment, we are just linking to the github where the designs reside.  More detailed instructions will follow.
